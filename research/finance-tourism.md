## 1. Market facts

### Supply: homestays
- WB state registry: >2,000 homestays registered (2022 Telegraph report); Kalimpong district 974 (about 300 more expected), Darjeeling about 200. [V] https://obeta.ttef.in/west-bengal/portal-on-homestays-to-help-tourists/cid/1875173
- National NIDHI+ rural-homestay registry (Aug 2025 Lok Sabha answer): West Bengal 570, Sikkim 16. [V] https://tourism.gov.in/sites/default/files/2025-08/usq.3344%20for%2021.08.pdf . The gap vs 2,000+ means different systems measure different things; real unregistered supply is likely larger. [?]
- Sikkim has its own homestay scheme and a large informal stock (the NIDHI+ count of 16 looks like under-registration, not true supply). [K] No Sikkim registry count found.
- WB Homestay Policy 2022 (3-year registration, DM office inspects before renewal) [V] https://wbxpress.com/west-bengal-homestay-tourism-policy-2022/ ; a "Final WB Homestay Policy 2026" PDF exists but with blank effective dates, status unconfirmed. [?] https://www.wbtourism.gov.in/assets/pdf/principal-notice/Final WB Homestay Policy 2026.pdf
- Implication: roughly 1,200 to 3,000 homestays in Darjeeling+Kalimpong that are formally registered. A direct-booking product does not need all of them; 50 good ones is a real business.

### How homestays get bookings today
- Mix of Booking.com, Airbnb, MakeMyTrip/Goibibo, Facebook pages, Instagram, WhatsApp, repeat guests, taxi drivers and local travel agents who bundle. Individual owners have their own simple sites (e.g. a Lovable-built "Mount Tea Stays" taking direct bookings at about Rs 1,600/night). [V partial] https://mountteastays.lovable.app/ ; the channel mix itself is [K].
- Payment: advance by UPI/bank transfer on WhatsApp is the norm for direct bookings; balance in cash at check-in. [K]

### OTA commission
- Booking.com about 15% (15-18%). [V, secondary] https://www.hostaway.com/blog/commission-rates-airbnb-vrbo/
- MakeMyTrip/Goibibo commonly 15-20%+; FHRAI complained of 18-40% in total deductions. [V, older] https://www.thestatesman.com/business/fhrai-puts-makemytrip-goibibo-notice-warns-nationwide-protest-issues-not-resolved-amicably-1502714077.html
- Airbnb: about 15.5% host-only or about 3% under split-fee; sources conflict. [?]
- Travel agents/package operators 20-25%+. [V, secondary]
- Plus GST on commission, plus 1% TCS (Sec 52 CGST) and TDS 1% (Sec 194-O) withheld by e-commerce operators from supplier payouts. [K]

### Transport
- NJP/Bagdogra to Darjeeling: prepaid counter fares about Rs 1,860 (small car) / Rs 2,400 (large) from older guides; fares last revised 2008 per a Bagdogra taxi association strike story; informal per-seat sharing at Bagdogra is officially disallowed, shared jeeps run from NJP/Siliguri (morning to mid-afternoon). [V, dated] https://www.darjeeling-tourism.com/darj_0000a4.htm , https://obeta.ttef.in/west-bengal/bagdogra-taxis-to-stay-off-road/cid/1803388
- Syndicate dynamics: Unions control the stands and prepaid counters; strikes and bandhs are a recurring risk; a tourist-facing booking product must work WITH unions (drivers book, you add payment/trust), not against them. [K]

### Treks, guides, permits
- Singalila/Sandakphu: entry about Rs 120/day Indian, Rs 500 foreigner; guide mandatory about Rs 1,000-1,300/day; porter Rs 800-1,000/day (sources differ). Permit via Manebhanjan forest office. [V, unofficial] https://www.lonelyplanet.com/india/darjeeling/attractions/singalila-national-park/a/poi-sig/1154785 , https://kwx.liehendr.com/content/wikivoyage_en_all_maxi_2025-02/A/Sandakphu
- Sikkim: Indians need no permit for most areas but need permits for North Sikkim/Nathu La/Tsomgo (via tour operator/vehicle); foreigners need Protected/Restricted Area Permits for specific zones. [K]
- Tea estates: Glenburn, Makaibari, Happy Valley, Temi offer paid stays/tours; Makaibari does homestay-style village stays. [K] Not researched further.

### Demand, seasonality, spend
- Peaks: Mar-May and Oct-Nov; monsoon Jul-Aug slack; winter quiet. [EST per research/quickfacts.md]
- No official district arrivals; Darjeeling foreign visits 2023 = 46,083 (Rajya Sabha). Toy train riders 2.08 lakh in 2025-26. [V via quickfacts.md]
- Typical domestic trip spend: guesses only, Rs 20-40k for a family of 4 over 3-4 nights (stay Rs 1.5-3k/night, cab Rs 2-6k, food, sightseeing). [?] Tata Capital has a trip cost page: https://tatacapital.com/blog/loan-for-travel/darjeeling-trip-cost

### Diaspora and remittances
- No Gorkha-specific diaspora count found. [V that it is missing]. Context: about 1.4M Nepalis in India's private/public sector plus about 700k abroad outside India [V, dated] https://www.migrationpolicy.org/node/4714 ; about 700k Nepali workers in UAE (all Nepalis, not Indian Gorkhas) [V] https://www.aa.com.tr/en/asia-pacific/iran-war-rattles-nepal-and-its-migrant-lifeline/3876114
- Indian Gorkha diaspora (Bengaluru, Delhi, Mumbai, Gulf, Sydney) is mostly hospitality/retail/BPO/security/nursing workers; sizeable but unquantified. [K] Money flow to parents is mostly UPI/bank transfer plus calls for "please arrange X" (doctor, cab, gas, fees). The need is arranging and verifying, not moving money; domestic UPI makes sending free. [K]

Key takeaway: money moving is solved (UPI). Trust, availability, and coordination in the hills are the unsolved parts.

## 2. Concepts

1. Direct-booking + UPI for hill homestays ("Ghar" listings, 5-8% fee vs 15-20% OTA).
2. Hill Pass: packaged itinerary (NJP pickup + homestay + toy train + tea tasting), sold as one payment, split to suppliers.
3. "Pay for Home" (diaspora): book cab, doctor visit, gas, groceries, school fee reminders for parents; verified local fixer executes.
4. Verified guide marketplace with escrowed deposits (Sandakphu, Sikkim, birding, food walks).
5. Tea garden experiences + direct tea D2C (stay/tour bookings, tea subscription).
6. Group trip split-pay for tourists (friends pool money, each pays share by UPI link, booking confirmed when full).
7. Homestay WhatsApp mini-SaaS (calendar, bookings, advance collection, GST-free simple invoices; Rs 499/month or per-booking).
8. Homestay working-capital lead-gen for partner NBFC (lead fee only, no lending).
9. Fair-price taxi index + prepaid booking with union-approved drivers (tourist-facing; drivers pay small fee).
10. Guide/creator tipping layer (UPI links; mostly no money through you).
11. Travel insurance distribution (POSP commission).
12. Own add: "Gift a Stay" / home-visit gift: diaspora buys a homestay stay for their own city friends (or parents' relatives) with dedication note, using music-room dedication mechanic. Voucher-based.
13. Own add: Festival/wedding season homestay blocks for diaspora families returning (Dashain/Tihar, Losar, weddings): group-block booking of 10-30 rooms with split payment.
14. The PvP game as a layer (drop for now, see §6).

Regulatory notes:
- Taking money for someone else's service: collecting through Razorpay/Cashfree payment links straight into the supplier's account, or using the gateway's marketplace split/route product, keeps you out of PA licensing; holding funds yourself in escrow does not. [K] Escrow, wallets and "balances" are the trap (PPI/PA-Cross-Border/PA rules). Use route/split + delayed settlement, never your own pooled account.
- Selling stays/packages in your own name makes you a tour operator/agent (WB tourism registration may apply; IATO is membership, not a licence) [K,?]; acting as agent that merely refers and takes a commission is lighter. GST: 5% on tour operator services, 18% on commission; e-commerce operator TCS Section 52 (1%) applies if you collect for suppliers as an ECO. [K]
- Insurance needs POSP/IRDAI-registered tie-up. Lending: only RBI-regulated LSP/DLG rules, referral lead-gen via NBFC's own app is the safe form. Tea-estate "investing/fractional ownership" is a SEBI collective investment scheme risk: drop. [K]
- DPDP Act: consent notices, parent/child data; closed-loop coins fine (per comparables.md). Real-money games banned (Online Gaming Act 2025). [V per project research]

## 4. Deep dives

### A. Festival and family group blocks + Gift a Stay + direct homestay booking, "Ghar Phirne" (concepts 13+12+1)
The core idea: gorkha.land becomes the place diaspora use to arrange a trip home (or send relatives/friends to the hills) and to book verified homestays directly. The music room already gets the emotion; this gives it a till.

Who pays: (a) diaspora families and city-based friend groups paying the room bill by UPI; (b) homestay owners pay a lower fee than OTAs.
Take rate: 6-8% from host, 0% guest fee (guess). Compare 15-20% OTA. Optional paid "block" coordination fee.
Unit economics (all GUESSES): average booking Rs 12,000 (2 rooms x 3 nights x Rs 2,000); 7% = Rs 840 gross; gateway about 2% = Rs 240 on the whole amount, which wipes much of it unless fee is net of gateway. So set 8% and negotiate Cashfree/Razorpay UPI rates (UPI MDR is zero for merchants in India, so use UPI links, not cards) [K,?]. Net about Rs 700/booking. 100 bookings/month = Rs 70k. Not a living until 300+/month. Festival blocks (20 rooms x 4 nights x Rs 1,800 = Rs 1.4L) at 8% = Rs 11k per block, better per unit of effort.
MVP in 4-6 weeks: Vite/React, Node/Postgres on one VPS. Weeks 1-2: 15-20 hand-onboarded homestays (visit/WhatsApp; you type in photos, rooms, prices, owner UPI); listing pages in Nepali + English; availability as a simple per-room blocked-dates table the owner updates by replying to a WhatsApp message (manual at first). Weeks 3-4: request-to-book form, owner confirms via WhatsApp link, guest pays 20-30% advance via Razorpay/Cashfree payment link or route split straight to host; you invoice only your commission. Week 5-6: group block page (share a link, each cousin pays their share), dedication note on booking confirmation ("Ama is hosting you" style). No inventory sync, no channel manager; calendar is the owner's reply.
How users find it: music-room dedication links add "send this song and a stay"; Instagram/Reels from the hills; WhatsApp community pushes before Dashain/Tihar and winter; local SEO "homestay in Kalimpong" long-tail; homestay owners share their own page link (their WhatsApp status is the distribution). 
Music/game feed: dedication link has a "come home" CTA; game zones become listing content ("play the Chowrasta zone, then stay at X").

### B. Pay for Home (diaspora errands) (concept 3)
Who pays: diaspora in Bengaluru/Delhi/Dubai for parents' needs: cab to hospital in Siliguri, a doctor appointment, gas cylinder, groceries, a puja booking, a driver for a relative's trip.
Take rate: Rs 100-300 flat per task or 10-15% over vendor price (guess); money often small (Rs 500-5,000 per task).
Economics (guesses): 200 tasks/month x Rs 150 = Rs 30k; labour heavy because each task needs a local fixer. Ops load is the problem: you need 2-3 trusted local fixers on retainer; works if a family member/partner operates it.
MVP: single WhatsApp number + web form "Kaam ghar ko"; pay by UPI link to you or direct to vendor; local fixer app is WhatsApp. Start with 3 services only: cab (prepaid at fixed rate), medical-visit escort, groceries/gas. Four weeks of web work, rest is ops recruiting.
Discovery: dedication flow (song for parents -> "also arrange something for them"). Strongest brand fit; weakest as a scalable money machine unless it later feeds tourism supply (fixers become guides/cab dispatchers).

### C. Homestay WhatsApp mini-SaaS (concept 7) as secondary/enabler
Who pays: host, Rs 299-999/month or per booking. Gives you the supply base for A cheaply and is low regulation (software only).
Guess: 60 paying hosts x Rs 499 = Rs 30k/month; churn high in off-season (Nov-Feb is peak for some, Jul-Aug dead). Cost to serve is low if the WhatsApp Business API is not used at the start (use click-to-chat links and a simple dashboard).
MVP: owner dashboard (calendar, add booking, collect advance link, auto-generated guest message in Hindi/Nepali/English). Four weeks. Risk: owners are unwilling to pay for software; likelier to pay as a commission only. Treat as a free tool that feeds A.

## 5. Competitors and gaps
- OTAs: Booking.com, MakeMyTrip, Goibibo, Airbnb, Agoda dominate search. Gaps: Nepali/local-language, local trust, high commission, no group-blocks.
- Zostel / Zostel Homes, Hosteller, Stayvista (premium), Treebo/OYO (mostly hotels): backpacker/premium positioning, not diaspora family. Zostel has Darjeeling/Kalimpong presence [K]; I could not verify via search.
- WB state homestay portal (wbtourism.gov.in listing portal for registered homestays) [V]: directory, little booking/payment, poor UX [K]. Official "Discover Darjeeling" app launched Oct 2026 [V per quickfacts.md] https://www.uniindia.com/east-india/tourism-wb-darjeeling-app/216720 ; unknown if bookable. Possible partner/feeder, not competitor, if it lists registered homestays.
- Sikkim Tourism portal and local travel agents/DMCs in Darjeeling, Gangtok, Siliguri: dominant for packages and permits. Gaps: opaque pricing, cash/UPI to personal numbers, trust issues for outsiders.
- Local direct sites like Mount Tea Stays: scattered, no shared trust/review layer.
- Gap summary: no diaspora-first, Nepali-language, trust+payment layer; no cross-homestay group block tool; no verified fair-price taxi bundle.

## 6. Recommendation

Primary: "Ghar Phirne": direct-booking for verified hill homestays, sold first to the diaspora via the music room, with family group blocks for Dashain/Tihar/winter and a Gift-a-Stay option (concepts 1+12+13). Rationale: highest money per use (Rs 10k+), fits the existing audience and share mechanic, can start with 15-20 hand-picked homestays, and avoids holding customer money (payment links/route-split straight to hosts). Keep it a referral/commission model; do not run escrow or sell packages in your own name until you have legal advice.

Secondary: Pay for Home, limited to 2-3 services and run as a concierge (WhatsApp + payment link), mainly because it creates the local fixer network and the brand story. Optionally free homestay WhatsApp tool as supply onboarding.

Drop or park from the current game plan:
- Merchant-funded offers/zone sponsorship as the revenue core: tiny ticket sizes and heavy sales load for Chowrasta shops.
- PvP territory mechanics and monthly resets: the cost is build/ops/moderation load; the money is not on this path. Keep only a light "stories at places" layer (audio guides, check-in) as content for listing pages and for the Discover Darjeeling gap.
- Diaspora "adopt a zone" funding: it overlaps with unlicensed pooling/crowdfunding questions; replace with Gift-a-Stay/Pay-for-Home.
- Tea-estate investments/fractional ownership (SEBI risk), your own escrow wallet, lending, and insurance until the base exists.

Next steps (before building): call 10 homestay owners (Kalimpong/Darjeeling/Mirik) and ask: current channels, what they pay, would they take a 7% direct-booking, who handles the phone; post a Dashain/Tihar interest poll to music-room users; ask a CA about GST/TCS and tour-operator status. These are the make-or-break validations.

Caveat: unit-economics numbers are guesses; the source base is thin, and Sikkim/NJP taxi/tea-estate/diaspora size facts remain unverified.
