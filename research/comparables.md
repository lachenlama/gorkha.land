# gorkha.land: Comparables Research
*Realtime location-based exploration + PvP game starting at Chowrasta, Darjeeling. Research date: 27 Oct 2026.*

> Legend: **[V]** = verified via a source during this research; **[K]** = well-established public knowledge (pre-2026), sources listed at the end; **[?]** = uncertain, still moving, or needs a lawyer to confirm.

---

## TL;DR
1. **Ingress is the template, not Pokémon GO.** Ingress gives you two-faction territory PvP and player-submitted POIs, and it runs on modest content. Pokémon GO needed a mega-IP. Turf (Sweden) shows that zone capture alone can sustain a niche community for over 10 years.
2. **POIs come from players, and players also review them.** Niantic's Wayfarer (submit, then peer review, then go live) built the world's best pedestrian POI database. It also became the asset worth selling: the games went to Scopely for $3.5B in 2025, while the spatial/map tech spun out as Niantic Spatial **[V]**.
3. **"Finance" means merchant value, not wagering.** India's Online Gaming Act 2025 bans anything where users stake money for a money-denominated return **[V]**. Every "earn real money" game (STEPN, Atlas Earth, Upland-style speculation) is either illegal or a reputational trap in India. Coupons, sponsored POIs, tipping guides and closed-loop points are the safe lane.
4. **In Darjeeling, identity is rocket fuel and a landmine.** The Gorkhaland sentiment will drive virality. But Darjeeling saw a roughly 100-day internet shutdown in 2017 **[K]**, and India blocks apps under Sec 69A without public reasons **[V]**. Go big on culture, music and nostalgia. Keep statehood and partisan politics out of the game mechanics.

| **Delta Force / military-sim GPS** | Mostly not location-based (Delta Force: Hawk Ops is a shooter) | n/a | n/a | n/a | Not relevant. Ignore. |

**Cross-cutting lessons**
- **POI sourcing.** Wayfarer's flow: a player at a high level submits a photo, title, description and supporting photo. Other high-level players review it against criteria (exploration, exercise, socializing; no private homes, schools or dangerous spots). Approved POIs feed every game. Contributors get no money, only status and influence over the map where they live. Wayfarer was part of the Scopely sale **[V]**, which shows how valuable the dataset is.
- **What drives retention:** (a) team identity, (b) scheduled synchronous events (Community Day, raid hours, Ingress anomalies), (c) daily streak/spin rewards, and (d) local chat groups. Gameplay depth matters much less.
- **What kills these games:** a confusing loop (HPWU), AR-first design (Minecraft Earth, Peridot), location that doesn't matter (JW Alive), GPS spoofing destroying PvP fairness, and lockdown/shutdown shocks (relevant to Darjeeling).

## 2. Real-map economy and finance-flavoured comps

| Product | Mechanic | Worked | Blew up / risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Upland** | Buy virtual deeds mapped to real addresses (UPX currency, NFTs), earn yield, collections | Real-street ownership is emotionally potent ("I own Mall Road"). | Speculation; value depends on new buyers; crypto/NFT regulatory exposure. In India this would collide with crypto tax (30% + 1% TDS) [K] and the gaming act. |
| **Atlas Earth** | Buy virtual parcels, earn tiny "rent", watch ads for boosts, cash out at $5 via PayPal **[V]** | Ad-funded "passive income" gets installs. | Rent is roughly **$0.10/parcel/year** **[V]**; widely criticized as an ad grind; rates can be cut at will. In India, "pay money, earn money" ≈ an **online money game, which is banned** **[?]/[V]**. |
| **Earth 2** | Buy 10m tiles of the planet; promised metaverse | Viral FOMO in 2021. | Under-delivered roadmap and lawsuits [K]. A cautionary tale for land speculation. |
| **Sweatcoin** | Steps convert to coins, which redeem for brand offers | Brand-funded rewards; huge installs; **no stake required**. | Coin devaluation; crypto (SWEAT) spin-off confusion. The **offer marketplace** is the part to copy. |
| **STEPN** | Buy NFT sneakers, walk, earn GST token | Explosive growth in 2022. | **Ponzi dynamics:** earnings depended on new buyers, and the token crashed more than 95%. China-user ban in 2022 [K]. *Avoid anything where earning requires buying in.* |
| **Monopoly GO** (Scopely) | Dice rolls, board, raids/shutdowns on friends, sticker albums, events | Social sabotage plus collection albums. Trading stickers drove viral sharing. Top-grossing mobile game of 2023–24 [K]. | Dice mechanics feel like gambling; regulators watch "loot box" mechanics. **Steal the sticker album and social raids, not the slot-machine feel.** |
| **Foursquare / Swarm** | Check-ins, **mayorships**, badges, tips | Mayorships = **territory through repeat visits**; tips = proto-reviews. Venue data became Foursquare's real business (B2B location data) [K]. | Gamification fatigue; split apps confused users. *The data was worth more than the game.* |
| **Google Local Guides** | Points for reviews, photos, edits, Q&A; levels 1–10; perks (historically Drive storage, meetups) | Millions of contributions, no pay; **status and levels** are enough. India is one of its largest communities [K]. | Spam and fake reviews; perks shrank over time. |
| **Zomato / Tripadvisor** | Foodie levels, badges, Travellers' Choice | Status, plus a merchant badge that venues display ("Certificate of Excellence") | Paid or fake reviews. India's **Consumer Protection (Fake/Deceptive Reviews) standard IS 19000:2022** discourages incentivized reviews unless disclosed [K]. |
| **India: UPI cashback / Paytm / PhonePe / CRED coins** | Scratch cards, cashback, coins redeemable for merchant deals; CRED "jackpot" | Indians are conditioned to scratch cards and coins. CRED coins are **closed-loop** (no cash out). | Paytm Payments Bank restricted by RBI in 2024 for compliance failures [V/K]. Cashback is funded by merchants or VCs; budgets dry up. |

**What this means:** the durable models are (1) **merchant-funded rewards** (Sweatcoin offers, CRED coins, PokéStop sponsorships), (2) **status for contributions** (Local Guides, Wayfarer, Foursquare mayors), and (3) **selling the location dataset or insights** B2B (Foursquare, Niantic Spatial). Every model where the user **pays in to earn out** either collapsed or is illegal in India.

## 3. Tourism, pocket tour guides, immersive trails

| Product | Model | Note |
|---|---|---|
| **GPSmyCity** | Offline self-guided walks; per-tour or subscription; user-written tours | A cheap catalog that scales on user tours. |
| **VoiceMap** | Local storytellers record GPS-triggered audio walks; **revenue share with authors**; B2B white-label for tourism boards | **Best model for Darjeeling:** local voices narrating, paid per tour. |
| **SmartGuide** | AI/TTS audio guides; freemium; deals with destinations | Shows how auto-generated narration lowers content cost. |
| **Detour** (Andrew Mason, shut 2018) | High-production GPS audio walks | **Failure:** expensive per-city content, low repeat use (you take a tour once), small market. Assets sold to Bose [K]. *Lesson: tours alone aren't a habit. Pair them with a repeatable game loop.* |
| **Questo / Actionbound / Let's Roam** | Puzzle scavenger hunts; per-hunt sales; Actionbound sells B2B licences to schools, museums and companies | Team-building and school trips are reliable B2B revenue. |
| **Google Arts & Culture** | Free; institution partnerships | Partners with museums/archives for prestige. Good partner model for **Darjeeling heritage archives** (Planters' Club, Bhanu Bhawan, HMI). |
| **Atlas Obscura** | UGC "hidden wonders" database, plus paid trips/experiences | A curated **UGC POI database that turns into paid experiences**. |
| **Komoot / AllTrails** | Route UGC; **premium subscription** (offline maps, regions); Komoot region packs | Hikers pay for offline maps. Relevant for Tiger Hill, Sandakphu, tea-garden trails. Komoot was sold to Bending Spoons in 2025 and cut staff [K/?]. |
| **India: Incredible India app, Audio Odigos, MakeMyTrip/Thrillophilia** | Government info apps; Audio Odigos sells per-monument audio guides; OTA marketplaces sell "experiences" with ~15–25% commission [?] | Government apps are a weak bar to clear. OTAs already list Darjeeling heritage walks (Yo Tours, Pelago) **[V]**. |
| **Darjeeling-specific** | West Bengal Tourism's **"Discover Darjeeling" app** launched Oct 2026 for circuits and trip planning **[V]**; human heritage walks ending with chai at Chowrasta **[V]** | The government app is an info catalog, not a game. **Partner with it rather than compete:** offer gorkha.land as the "play" layer or a feed of POIs. |

**Business-model menu:** per-tour sales (VoiceMap, GPSmyCity), B2B white-label for tourism boards and hotels, sponsored POIs, school and corporate hunts (Actionbound), premium offline maps (Komoot), and experience commissions (Thrillophilia).

## 4. Cultural / identity-driven games and political risk

| Title | What it shows |
|---|---|
| **Liyla and the Shadows of War** (Gaza) | Apple initially refused to list it as a "game" because of its "political" subject. After a public outcry it was allowed in the Games category [K]. **Stores police "games" about real conflicts.** |
| **The Revolution of Our Times** (HK protest game, 2020) | **Removed from Google Play** for "capitalising on sensitive events… serious ongoing conflicts" **[V]**. |
| **HKmap.live** (2019) | Crowd-sourced police-location map; **Apple removed it** after Chinese state media pressure, citing danger to law enforcement **[V]**. *Directly relevant: a crowd-sourced live map of a contested place, where users report positions, is exactly the feature that gets pulled.* |
| **Cytus II / Reversed Front** | One coded "Liberate Hong Kong" slogan got a game pulled from mainland stores **[V]**. In 2025, HK police banning *Reversed Front: Bonfire* made it go viral **[V]**: suppression amplifies, but you lose the market. |
| **Unrest** (India-set RPG) | A Kickstarter indie set in an ancient-India-inspired world with famine and caste politics; well received because the politics was **systemic and fictionalized**. |
| **Raji: An Ancient Epic** | Indian mythology and Balinese/Rajasthani art; global acclaim. Culture as **aesthetic pride**, not partisan politics. |
| **Venba** | Tamil immigrant family cooking game; BAFTA-winning [K]. **Diaspora nostalgia = global virality.** Very close to gorkha.land's music-site roots. |
| **Mukti** (anti-trafficking, India) | Social-issue game; credibility via NGO partnerships. |
| **Kashmir / Northeast India-themed games** | Few exist; most stay arthouse because of the risk. Indian authorities have blocked content on Kashmir and Manipur (internet shutdowns, X account blocks) [K]. |
| **Fauda / military shooters** | Real conflicts as entertainment trigger boycotts from the "other side". |

**Darjeeling-specific context:** Gorkhaland is an active statehood demand. The 2017 agitation brought a ~104-day general strike and a **state-ordered internet shutdown in the hills** [K]. Section 69A blocking orders are **confidential and need no public justification** **[V]**. PUBG, Free Fire and BGMI were all blocked under it **[V]**. Combined with a hyperlocal map of a politically contested district, that is real platform risk.

**Lessons**
- **Use** the identity: Nepali language, music (the site's heritage), food (momo, thukpa), festivals (Dashain, Tihar, Losar), tea gardens, Toy Train, Gorkha regimental pride, and diaspora longing. This is the Venba/Raji lane, and it travels well.
- **Avoid:** factions named after political parties or movements; maps of "Gorkhaland" borders; live crowd-reporting of police, protests or bandhs; any mechanic that could be read as mobilization. Make factions **neutral and playful** (e.g., *Kanchenjunga vs Tiger Hill*, or tea-estate houses, or fantasy clans).
- **Have a "bandh mode":** if the internet is shut down, the game freezes territory decay so locals aren't punished. This is also a strong goodwill gesture.
- **Self-hosting on a VPS** doesn't protect you from 69A: blocking orders go to ISPs and app stores. A **PWA on your own domain** does survive store takedowns better.

## 5. India regulatory snapshot (Oct 2026)

**5.1 Promotion and Regulation of Online Gaming Act, 2025 (PROGA)**
- Passed Aug 2025. **Bans "online money games"**: games where users pay fees or deposit money or stakes **expecting money or other monetary enrichment**, whether skill or chance. It also bans advertising them and banks/payment systems processing them. Penalties go up to **3 years in jail and ₹1 crore** **[V]**.
- **Promotes e-sports and "online social games"** (no stakes, no monetary winnings). A regulator, the **Online Gaming Authority of India (OGAI)**, sits under MeitY **[V]**.
- **Rules:** draft published 2 Oct 2025 **[V]**; final rules notified **22 Apr 2026**, reportedly effective **1 May 2026** **[V]**. Social-game **registration is voluntary or only for notified categories**; e-sports registration is mandatory **[V]**. A code of conduct may later cover **micro-transactions** **[V]**. Three-tier grievance redressal **[V]**.
- **[?] Grey zones:** (a) whether **in-game currency that can be bought with money and also redeemed for things of monetary value** (vouchers) makes a game a "money game"; (b) whether paid entry to a contest with merchant prizes counts as a stake. **Keep the purchase path and the reward path separate.**

**5.2 DPDP Act 2023 + DPDP Rules 2025**
- Rules notified **13/14 Nov 2025** **[V]**. Phasing: Data Protection Board provisions immediately; consent managers at 12 months (~Nov 2026); **substantive obligations at 18 months (~13 May 2027)** **[V]**, including notice and consent, security, breach notification and **verifiable parental consent for under-18s** **[V]**.
- **Children:** under 18 = child. You need verifiable parental consent, and **no tracking, behavioural monitoring or targeted ads at children** (Act s.9) [K]. Precise realtime location of minors is the riskiest data you could hold. **Recommendation: 18+ for PvP/live location at launch, or a kid mode with coarse location, no live presence and no chat.**
- **Location data** isn't a special category under DPDP, but it needs purpose-specific consent, data minimization, deletion when the purpose is done, and breach reporting. **Don't publish other players' live positions**; show territory owners, not people. Store coarse or decayed trails only.
- **[?]** Significant Data Fiduciary designation (DPIAs, audits) is unlikely at this scale.

**5.3 RBI: payments, wallets, points**
- **Closed-system PPIs** (value usable only for the issuer's own goods/services, **no cash-out, no third-party payments**) are **not a payment system needing RBI authorization** **[V]**. In-game coins usable only in-game fall here.
- Once points are **redeemable at third-party merchants** or for cash, you move into semi-closed PPI territory, which needs an RBI licence and net worth requirements [K]. **Safe pattern:** merchants issue *their own* coupons (merchant-funded discount codes) and your points just unlock them. Never let users cash out points or transfer them P2P.
- **Tipping guides:** route through **UPI deep links / merchant QR directly to the guide's VPA**. You never hold funds, so you aren't a payment aggregator. If you take a commission, use a licensed PA (Razorpay/Cashfree) **[?]**.

**5.4 GST**
- Non-money games: in-app purchases and subscriptions carry **18% GST** [K]. Real-money gaming carried 28% on deposits from Oct 2023; GST 2.0 (Sept 2025) reportedly moved it to a 40% slab [?]. That's moot now since it's banned.
- Tour sales, guide commissions and sponsored-POI advertising are ordinary services at **18%**. Registration is mandatory above ₹20 lakh turnover; **OIDAR/e-commerce operator rules** may require registration from day one if you collect for third parties [?].

**5.5 App store payments**
- Google was fined **₹936 crore by the CCI (2022)** and offers **User Choice Billing** in India at reduced fees. Apple is under CCI investigation [K/?]. Digital goods inside store apps must use store billing (15–30%). **Physical-world goods and services** (tours, merchant coupons, tips) **can use external payments**. Running a **PWA from your VPS** sidesteps store fees for web users.

**Plain statement: what "finance" may and may not be in gorkha.land**

| ✅ Safe(ish) | ⚠️ Lawyer first | ❌ Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Merchant coupons unlocked by play; sponsored POIs/zones; paid tours; tips to guides via UPI deep link; cosmetic IAP; season pass; B2B dashboards for the tourism board; "mayor of the momo shop" status | Points redeemable for third-party vouchers; paid tournament entry with sponsor prizes; points transferable between users; affiliate commission on bookings | Pay-to-earn; buy virtual land/property that earns rent; cash-out of game currency; crypto tokens/NFTs; any wager, lottery or loot box sold for money with cash-value prizes |

### 6.2 Product recommendations

1. **Smallest viable PvP loop ("Turf + Ingress lite").** Divide Chowrasta, the Mall, Chowk Bazaar, the Toy Train station, Batasia Loop and the zoo/HMI into ~50 hex or POI zones. Standing within ~40 m for 30–60 s **captures** a zone for your faction. You earn capture points plus **points per hour held**. Three adjacent zones form a "field" with a bonus. **Monthly season reset.** Factions are neutral (e.g., *Kanchenjunga* vs *Tiger Hill*; add a third later if one dominates). Nothing else is in v1: no combat, no inventory, no AR.
2. **Anti-cheat from day one.** Server-authoritative captures; plausibility checks on speed and teleport; reject mock locations on Android; zone lockout timers; rate limits. Spoofers kill PvP in a small town far faster than in a megacity.
3. **Wayfarer-lite POI pipeline.** Players at level 5+ submit a POI (photo, name, story in Nepali/English, category). **3 trusted reviewers** approve it, and only then does it go live. Use the criteria "explorable, safe, public, not a private home/school/religious interior". Reward accepted POIs with permanent attribution ("discovered by …"), badges and voting weight. Seed the first ~150 POIs yourself from OpenStreetMap and heritage lists.
4. **Every POI is also a review and tour stop.** Capture unlocks a 3-line story and **audio clip** (VoiceMap-style, recorded by locals: old-timers, musicians, guides) plus micro-reviews (Swarm tips). The **music-site heritage** becomes the content: zone soundtracks with Darjeeling songs (cleared rights) tied to places.
5. **Safe "finance" layer (merchant-funded).** (a) Sponsored zones: a café or momo shop pays a monthly fee to be a zone with a branded tile, and holders unlock its coupon. (b) A "mayor" of a shop gets a real perk the merchant funds. (c) **Tip-a-guide** via UPI deep link to the guide's VPA. (d) Paid premium tours and group hunts. Game coins are closed-loop, earned only, non-transferable, no cash-out. No buying land, no yields.
6. **Diaspora mode (remote play).** Diaspora in Bengaluru, Dubai or Sydney can't capture zones in Darjeeling but can **adopt a zone** (cosmetic, name on the tile), fund a merchant coupon for a local player ("buy a chai for whoever holds Chowrasta"; legally this is a gift voucher, so check it **[?]**), listen to place stories and vote on POI submissions. Optionally they can run their *own* hometown-style zones where they live. This turns the remote audience into revenue and engagement without breaking location fairness.
7. **Privacy-first design for DPDP.** Show **territory, not people**; no live player dots; trails coarsened to zone level and deleted after the season; 18+ for PvP at launch; consent screens in Nepali, English and Hindi; a data deletion button. Have this done before the May 2027 deadline.
8. **Political safety rails.** Neutral faction names; moderation of POI names and descriptions (ban party symbols, border maps, protest-site reporting); **bandh/shutdown freeze** of decay; a published content policy. Ship as a **PWA on your VPS** plus store apps so a store takedown isn't fatal.
9. **Hyperlocal go-to-market (~120k residents + tourists).**
   - *Phase 0, Chowrasta only.* Recruit 30–50 founders (college students at St. Joseph's/Loreto/Govt College, musicians from the site's audience, football clubs). Run a weekly "Chowrasta Hour" capture battle on Saturday evenings; the Mall is already the town's social square.
   - *Phase 1, festivals.* Dashain/Tihar and the **Darjeeling Carnival** as Community Days; a Toy Train route "raid"; a Tiger Hill sunrise capture.
   - *Phase 2, tourists.* QR posters at hotels, homestays and taxi stands ("Play Darjeeling, unlock a free chai"). Partner with **Discover Darjeeling** (WB Tourism) and DGHA/GTA tourism for an official "heritage trail" layer. Sell **group hunts** to school trips and corporate offsites from Kolkata and Siliguri.
   - *Phase 3, nearby towns.* Kalimpong, Kurseong, Mirik and Gangtok, plus diaspora cities. Each town gets a monthly zone budget like Turf's municipality model.
10. **The long-term moat is the dataset.** User-validated POIs, foot-traffic heat (aggregated and anonymized), and Nepali-language reviews of hill places are data no one else has. Monetize them B2B (tourism board dashboards, merchant footfall insights), the Foursquare/Niantic Spatial path, only with aggregate, consented data.
