# गोर्खाल्याण्ड / gorkha.land — how we write Nepali

**Follow this file for every line of Nepali in the app, the website, notifications, social posts and printed
material.** gorkha.land writes the Nepali of the Darjeeling hills (Darjeeling, Kurseong,
Kalimpong, Mirik and the Dooars), **not Kathmandu Nepali.** Kathmandu Nepali is correct
in Nepal. Here it reads as written *about* us, not *by* us.

When in doubt, ask: *would someone from Chowk Bazaar say this out loud?* If it sounds like
a Nepal news bulletin or a textbook, rewrite it.

---

## 1. Identity rules (these don't change)

- **Never use Nepal's calendar.** No Bikram Sambat dates ("Kartik 2083", २०८३, etc.).
  Use the ordinary Gregorian date.
- **Never present the hills as part of Nepal.** The Nepali language is ours. Nepal's
  national markers (its flag, its calendar, its places as "home") are not.
- **Identity, not agitation** ([design.md §9](design.md)): no party names, slogans, or
  demands.
- **The hills belong to everyone who lives here:** Nepali, Lepcha, Bhutia, Tibetan,
  Bengali. Don't write as if only one group is speaking.

## 2. Word list

| Kathmandu Nepali ❌ | Darjeeling ✅ | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| पसल | **दोकान** | shop |
| कुहिरो / हुस्सु | **कुइरो** | fog / mist |
| चिया पकाउनु | **चिया बनाउनु** | make tea |
| सिपाही | **फौजी** | soldier |
| युवा / युवायुवती | **ठिटाठिटी** | young people |
| अर्थतन्त्र | **काम-धन्दा** (in everyday writing) | economy, livelihood |
| परदेश | **बाहिर** / **बाहिर कतै** | away from home, abroad |
| बेङ्गलुरु | **बेङ्गलोर** | Bangalore |
| प्रिय (to a place or a group) | **प्यारो** | dear |
| शुभ प्रभात / शुभ सन्ध्या / शुभ रात्रि | **नमस्ते** (or just "Good morning" in English) | greetings |
| …नुहोस् (जानुहोस्, लेख्नुहोस्) | **…नुस्** (जानुस्, लेख्नुस्) | polite command, in buttons and prompts |

**Words both Nepal and the hills use, which are fine:** हामी यहीँका हौँ, धन्यवाद, झरी, मोटर
स्ट्यान्ड, चौरस्ता, डाँडा, बाटो, घर, आमा, चिया, मोमो, जमघट, गफ, घर याद आयो.

**When you find a new word:** add it to this table with the Kathmandu version beside it.
Don't change one screen on its own and leave the rest inconsistent.

## 3. Register: how formal to be, by place

| Where | Register | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **The letter, About page, legal summaries** | Warm, plain hill Nepali, in full sentences. Not Sanskritised. | एउटा कुरा लेखेर जानुस्। हामी हरेक कुरा पढ्छौँ। |
| **App screens, buttons, notifications** | Short. Roman Nepali mixed with English is fine. Devanagari for product names. | "Check in garnus", "Crew le Kunga hold garyo 🔥" |
| **Instagram, reels, share cards** | Gen Z hill slang (see §4). Playful, never mean. | "Mams, aaja kun crew heavy?" |
| **Legal text** (privacy, terms, café agreement) | Clear English, plus a short summary in plain hill Nepali | — |

**Script choice:**
- **Devanagari** for product names (जमघट, काम, हाट, बास), the logo, and anything heartfelt.
- **Roman Nepali** for everyday UI and social posts, because that's how young people in
  the hills type.

## 4. Gen Z hill slang ("thegos")

These come from [Gorkhapedia's list of Darjeeling thegos](http://gorkhapedia.wikidot.com/the-thegos-og-darjeeling).

**OK on social media and in casual app copy:**

| Word | Use |
|---|---|
| **mams** / **bro** | Friends addressing each other ("O mams, kata?") |
| **heavy** | Impressive, great ("Heavy crew!") |
| **satis** | Just right, satisfied ("Yo café ko momo satis") |
| **jhingo** | Talented, admired |
| **choora** / **kiraa** | A master at something ("Quiz ko kiraa") |
| **the** | An exceptional event ("Hijo Chowrasta ma THE bhayo") |
| **senti** | Overly emotional, used teasingly |

**Never in our copy:**
- Anything about alcohol or drugs: khatu, masta, lapaax, saap, chirip, kada, raato,
  bambs.
- Threats: "chha inch".
- Words that mock someone's looks or background: paakhey, koday.

The app is 16+ and our partners are cafés, so leave these out.

## 5. Spelling

- **Open question:** Nepal's standard spelling uses the chandrabindu (छौँ, हौँ). Some
  Indian Nepali writing uses the anusvara (छौं, हौं). We use the **chandrabindu** for
  now. Change it everywhere at once if a reviewer from the hills says otherwise.
- Use the place names people here use: दार्जिलिङ, खरसाङ, कालेबुङ, मिरिक, सिलगढी. Use
  "Darjeeling" in English. Never write दार्जीलिङ in one place and दार्जिलिङ in another.
- Write numbers in Latin digits (2026, 5G, 736), not Devanagari digits, in all copy.

## 6. Review before anything ships

Before any Nepali goes live:
1. Check it against this file.
2. **Have one native speaker from the hills read it aloud.** If it sounds like Nepal TV,
   rewrite it.
3. Add any new words to §2.
